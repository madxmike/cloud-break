The cloud

  An evaporating cloud states a conflict as five statements:

    A   the objective both sides share
    B   a need of the first side          C   a need of the second side
    D   what the first side wants         D'  what the second side wants

  A-B, A-C, B-D and C-D' are necessity arrows: "in order to <upper>, we must
  <lower>". D and D' collide, and that collision is the conflict.

  A cloud is only worth working on when all five statements are filled in and D
  and D' actually disagree. check reports an empty statement as an error, and a
  D' that only negates D as info.

Statements:
  set accepts the slot names A, B, C, D and D'. D2 is accepted for D', because
  a bare apostrophe is awkward to quote in a shell. An empty text clears the
  statement. Statements are collapsed to one line.

Arrows:
  assume add accepts A-B, A-C, B-D, C-D' and D-D'. The hyphen is optional and
  D2 stands in for D', so C-D', C-D2 and CD2 all name the same arrow.

  D-D' carries the conflict itself: assumptions on it are the reasons the two
  wants cannot both be satisfied.

Assumptions:
  An assumption is a "because" behind an arrow: the unstated claim the arrow
  depends on. Every arrow hides some, and the cloud evaporates when an
  assumption is invalidated.

  An assumption has a text, a verdict and optional evidence:

  verdict       unassessed  not judged yet (the default)
                holds       there is cause and evidence for it
                questionable  plausible, but not established
                invalid     it does not hold

  assume verdict also accepts unknown or none for unassessed, hold or true for
  holds, question for questionable, and false for invalid.

  The verdict is a judgement about the assumption, not about the person holding
  it. Mark one invalid when you can show it does not hold, and say what breaks
  it in the evidence field.

Injections:
  An injection is the change that makes an assumption irrelevant: the
  breakthrough that dissolves the conflict instead of trading one want against
  the other. An injection names the assumptions it breaks, so the link is
  recorded from both ends.

Process:
  The five stages are derived from the cloud itself, so no stage has to be
  ticked off by hand. show and check report where a cloud stands:

  1  State the conflict           all five statements are filled in
  2  Surface the assumptions      every arrow has at least one assumption
  3  Find the weak link           an assumption has been judged invalid
  4  Inject a breakthrough        an injection breaks that assumption
  5  Verify the cloud evaporates  you confirmed the injection dissolves it

Status:
  A cloud's status follows the same evidence:

  draft         the statements are not complete
  open          complete, and nothing has fallen yet
  dissolving    an assumption is invalid, but no verified injection breaks it
  dissolved     an injection breaks an invalid assumption, and you verified it

  Nothing is marked dissolved on your behalf: verify is the step where a person
  says the conflict is gone, and verify --undo takes it back.

Validation:
  check reports three severities. error blocks: a missing statement, D and D'
  saying the same thing, an arrow joining an empty statement. warning means the
  cloud is inconclusive: an arrow with no assumptions, an injection that breaks
  nothing or breaks an assumption judged to hold, an injection pointing at an
  assumption that no longer exists, blank text. info is a hint: D' only negates
  D, an invalid assumption nothing injects against yet.
